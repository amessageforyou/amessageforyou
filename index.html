[About]

Self-proclaimed "Melodically deranged, emotional alternative bullshit.". "A Message for You (Often shortened to "AMFY"), is a Post-Hardcore/Alternative metal project based in Edmonton, Alberta. Formed by Damien Raine at 17 years old in May 2026. Influenced by bands like [Minus] (aka Minus.Driver), Deftones, Deadly Medley/Delicious Red, Concision, Loathe, Addiction 96, and more. On January 25, 2026, "House In Home" was released as a single under a project named "Pilfered", originally intended to be made for an alias titled "C-"/"C Minus". After that, the song was forgotten about. Until the last 2 weeks of May, Damien went back down to his drop Ab/Eb standard hybrid tuning, and 5 songs were written, as well as an experimental minute-long track. Vulva was written throughout the period of May 14-19, It released as a single on the 19th with House In Home as the second track. The single was titled "<3"."

Hi, my name is Damien, I am 17. I Started this project in mid May of this year. I'm not some website prodigy so dont mind the poor quality. Here I'm just gonna be dumping information, kinda how I would on my instagram story. im gonna stop worrying about my grammar now cuz im lazy. I do not use ai, ai is for cucks and poo poo dum dums who would rather see robots "make" "art" than humans. I use the DAW Reaper, and i Write all my drums in person, but do not have mics, i translate my writing to a midi drum kit. Everything i write is playable, everything i write i can also play. I own a schecter c-7 diamond series 7 string, which half of my most recent album was recorded on. The first EP was recorded on a BC Rich the Fly warlock. You can see both guitars used on my youtube. I dont know what model by bass is but its a shitty ibanez. I use a line 6 amp, which i have spent too many hours tone making. For potential live shows where i have to bring an amp, ill probably bring my brand X x-25r, with my amazon chorus pedal and boss Ds-1. I also use a yahama acoustic electric sometimes for my acoustic tracks. I mix, master, write, and record everything by myself from my bedroom. I am not rich, but i do the best with what i have. I am also a general producer, i offer free mixing services for full band work, demos, albums, whatever, just email me. I also write other music than rock, ive done hip hop, drum and bass, industrial, and more. I often like to take my electronic influence for music, and bend it to fit my music. Like in the song "Sweetheart Neckline", it uses synths, and such, but not in ways that would normally work. On my newest album, there are many experimental ambient tracks.

Here's my music. All of my music is free to download. I do not believe in paying for digital music, which you can just pirate. I believe in donations. Feel free to donate, itll help for future releases.
